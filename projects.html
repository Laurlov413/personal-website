Lost Meadows

Mountain meadows are essential to water storage, carbon sequestration, and biodiversity, but many are degrading due to climate change and historical land-use practices. This project used environmental data and machine learning to identify degraded meadows across the Cascade-Siskiyou Bioregion, helping natural resource managers make data-driven decisions about restoration priorities.
